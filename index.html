About Us

Who we are

Student-led risk & investment club at LESCO, passionate about bridging theory and practice.

Established: 2024 to create a platform for risk-aware investing in Lesotho.

What makes us different

We combine risk management with investment strategy and connect it to real African & global contexts. Plus, we offer a dedicated member portal.

Vision & Mission

Vision: A financially resilient Africa.
Mission: Develop risk-intelligent investment leaders through education, events, and applied research.

Objectives

  • Promote risk literacy
  • Build investment skills
  • Connect students with industry
  • Produce research on African markets

Constitution & Governance

Approved constitution

Available for download in member portal. The club operates under a democratic structure with elected executives.

Club rules: Regular attendance, participation in at least 2 events per semester, and adherence to ethical investment principles.

Membership requirements: Full-time student at LESCO, interest in risk/investment, and payment of annual fee (M100).

Members' rights: Vote in elections, access learning materials, participate in all events. Responsibilities: Uphold club values, contribute to discussions, respect confidentiality.
