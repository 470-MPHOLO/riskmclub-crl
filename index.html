About Us

Who we are

Student-led risk and investment club at Botho University, passionate about bridging theory and practice.

Established: to create a platform for risk-aware investing in Africa.

What makes us different

We combine risk management with investment strategy and connect it to real African and global contexts. Structured governance, clear communication, strong member benefits, and professional partnerships.

Vision

Empower students to become leaders in finance and risk management.

Mission

Provide education, mentorship, and opportunities in risk management and investment.

Objectives

  • Risk management and investment education
  • Workshops and seminars
  • Career development and networking
  • Research and competitions
  • Industry discussions

Why Join Us

We live in a world shaped by financial uncertainty, cybercrime, climate change, AI disruption, and economic instability. Our club equips students to identify, assess, and respond to these challenges while building their future through leadership and connection.

Constitution and Governance

Approved Constitution

The club operates under a democratic structure with elected executives. Available for download in member portal.

Eligibility

Open to all Botho University students interested in risk management, finance, insurance, entrepreneurship, leadership, and professional development.

Rights of Members

  • Attend meetings and activities
  • Vote in elections
  • Contest leadership positions
  • Propose ideas and recommendations
  • Access club programs and opportunities

Duties of Members

  • Respect the constitution and decisions
  • Actively participate in activities
  • Promote unity, professionalism, and teamwork
  • Protect the reputation of the club and university
  • Pay approved membership contributions
  • Support initiatives and events

Termination of Membership

Membership may be suspended or terminated for misconduct, repeated violations, harassment, or bringing the club or university into disrepute.

Membership

Who can join

All Botho University students (undergraduate and postgraduate) with an interest in risk, finance, insurance, entrepreneurship, leadership, or investment.

Benefits

  • Professional networking with industry leaders
  • Skill acquisition in risk frameworks, governance, and compliance
  • Academic and career support through study circles, resume workshops, and internship alerts
  • Leadership development via sub-committees and administrative roles
  • Access to member portal and learning resources

Requirements

  • Active Botho University student status
  • Digital application form submission
  • Attendance of at least 70% of meetings and workshops per semester

Financial Commitment: Annual membership fee of M100 to support operations and events.

Communication Channels: Direct email newsletters, WhatsApp group for instant updates, web portal for announcements and schedules.
